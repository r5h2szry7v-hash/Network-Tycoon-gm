    Quick start

    How to tinker

    1. Choose a part from the tray, then tap an empty workbench square to place it.
    2. Tap a placed part to rotate it. Use Remove part to return pieces to your tray.
    3. Connect the marble at the left to the bell on the right, then run the machine.
    4. Belt: carries the marble straight. Ramp: turns it through a right angle. Switch: offers two exit branches. Gear: redirects the marble according to its orientation. Spring: launches it straight across one square to the next part.
    5. Place several parts of the selected type, rotate placed parts, or switch on Remove part to return one to the tray.
    6. Clear a puzzle to earn a hint. Hints can point to the next part or the next rotation.
    7. Use Parts guide from the workshop note whenever you want to review each part.
