How to play

Each number tells you how many danger patches touch that tile, including diagonals.

👣Clear trail to reveal a tile and open connected clear ground.
🚩Mark danger when clues tell you a patch is hidden there.
🏡Connect the trail home by linking the starting tile, all three friends, and the glowing home marker.

On touch screens, switch to Mark danger before tapping a tile you think is hazardous.
