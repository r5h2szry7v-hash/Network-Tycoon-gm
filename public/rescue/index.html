💡How to play

Each number tells how many danger patches touch that tile, including diagonals.

👣Clear trail to reveal a tile and open connected clear ground.
🚩Mark danger when clues tell you a patch is hidden there.
🏡Bring all friends home by clearing every safe tile.

Tip: Switch to Mark danger before tapping tiles on touch screens.

Hints and scans are optional assists. They are tracked separately and do not change your rescue progress.
