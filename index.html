  1. 🗺️
    Choose a district

    Open Map and pick an unserved neighborhood. Compare its homes, competition, and build cost.

  2. 🛠️
    Build your connection

    Choose Wide Wireless, Classic Coax, or Fast Fiber. Check cost, capacity, and build time before confirming.

  3. 📊
    Focus on what matters

    On the Board, “Act now” flags urgent work; “Can wait” keeps optional moves handy. Monthly Recap tracks your progress.

  4. ⏱️
    Advance at your pace

    Choose Next Month for control or Play to keep moving. The game pauses when an important decision needs you.

Explore connected districts

Discoveries are clickable in active districts. Find shops, rewards, and the staffing agency after establishing service in three regions. Track supplies in Inventory.

🎯
Play to win new territory

Fresh match-3, Battleship, and hidden-object bids award 0, 2, 4, or 6 districts. The timer starts at two minutes, drops by 15 seconds every three bids, and bottoms out at one minute. Pay the award cost only if you accept.

🏦
Use capital carefully

Borrowing power is the higher of $100,000 or 20% of cash, with up to three active loans. Acquisition offers show the cash-scaled price and the target’s risk.

🛡️
Prepare for major incidents

After a month 7 heads-up, the first incident can arrive from month 8 when at least four districts are operating and $50,000 is available. Later incidents require a wider or aging network, stay at least 18 months apart, and keep the same type out of rotation for 30 months. Each incident offers a response plan; recovery time grows with its severity.

☀️ New to tycoon games? Casual mode offers more room to learn; Balanced raises the stakes.🏆 Complete all five victory goals to unlock Endless Mode.
